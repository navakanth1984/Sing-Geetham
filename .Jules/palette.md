## 2024-07-18 - Ensuring accessibility on highly interactive icon-only buttons
**Learning:** In Vite/Tailwind applications focusing on heavy interactivity (like mock calling interfaces and live sessions), icon-only buttons are commonly used for design minimalism. However, missing ARIA labels on these elements heavily degrades the screen reader experience. Similarly, inputs without explicitly linked `<label>` or `aria-label` suffer from poor a11y context. Furthermore, custom styling easily overrides default focus behaviors resulting in poor keyboard navigability.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons and unlabelled inputs have descriptive `aria-label`s. Ensure `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-amber-500` (or similar brand colors) is consistently applied to all interactive elements to preserve clear keyboard-driven UX paths across the app.
