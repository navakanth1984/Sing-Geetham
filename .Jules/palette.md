## 2026-07-03 - App-wide inaccessible icon-only buttons and unlabelled inputs
**Learning:** Found a systemic pattern across multiple components (`App.tsx`, `LiveSessionJam.tsx`) where icon-only buttons (like Sign Out and Send) rely solely on `title` attributes or lack them completely, rather than using proper `aria-label`s. Furthermore, several inputs/selects lacked explicit `<label>` elements or `aria-label` attributes, and keyboard `focus-visible` states were either missing or incorrectly relying on hover/focus border changes instead of standard ring outlines.
**Action:** Always ensure newly implemented icon-only buttons have an `aria-label` and form inputs have explicit labelling. Implement `focus-visible:ring-2` for keyboard navigation accessibility to be consistent with standard a11y practices.
