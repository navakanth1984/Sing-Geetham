## 2026-06-29 - Interactive Element Accessibility Enhancements\n**Learning:** Many icon-only action buttons across the app (like download, like, and sign out buttons) rely solely on visual cues or 'title' attributes, which can be inaccessible to screen readers. Furthermore, focus rings are missing, making keyboard navigation difficult.\n**Action:** Add 'aria-label' to all icon-only buttons and implement consistent 'focus-visible:ring-2' states for proper keyboard accessibility.
